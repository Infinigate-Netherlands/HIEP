Collaboration

Why Infinigate supports HIEP

Secure digital identity helps healthcare organizations work together. Infinigate supports HIEP as a place to share knowledge and develop reusable architectures and standards.

The aim is vendor-neutral collaboration on interoperability between healthcare organizations, government and technology partners.

HIEP is a collaborative knowledge project, not a commercial Infinigate product. Published material should be assessed for its stated scope and maturity.
